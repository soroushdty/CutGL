Inhaler Technique Review for Adults With Asthma

Example guideline. The text is invented to demonstrate GEM Cutter and is not clinical guidance.

Version 2, released June 2026 by the Example Respiratory Care Group.

Scope

This guideline applies to adults aged 18 years or older with a diagnosis of asthma who use an inhaler. It does not apply to people who use a nebuliser only.

How the guideline was developed

A panel of two nurses, one pharmacist and one physician reviewed studies published between 2015 and 2025 and agreed the recommendations by consensus.

Recommendations

  1. A nurse or pharmacist should watch the patient use their inhaler at every asthma review. (Strong recommendation, moderate-quality evidence)
  2. If the patient makes an error in technique, the clinician should demonstrate the correct technique and ask the patient to repeat it. (Strong recommendation, low-quality evidence)
  3. If the patient cannot use a pressurised metered-dose inhaler correctly after teaching, the clinician should offer a spacer. (Conditional recommendation, very low-quality evidence)

Definitions

TermMeaning
SpacerA holding chamber attached to a metered-dose inhaler.
Asthma reviewA planned visit to assess control, treatment and technique.
