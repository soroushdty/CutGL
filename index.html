          CutGL, Cutter for Guidelines
          
  
          An online port of GEM Cutter III, the Guideline Elements Model authoring tool from the Yale Center for Medical Informatics (2012). It shows GEM III documents beside the guideline they were cut from, builds new ones, and opens project folders made with the desktop version.
          
  
          The GEM III schema and the six report stylesheets are the ones shipped with the desktop application. Everything runs in this page: the guideline and your markup stay in your browser, and projects you work on are kept in this browser's storage until you save them as a .zip.
          
  
          Projects saved here can be unzipped next to GemCutter.jar and opened in the desktop application. The sample projects use invented text and are not clinical guidance.
